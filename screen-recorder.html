📌 How to add screen recording to your course:

  1. Record: Click "Start Recording" → Choose your screen → Present your content
  2. Stop: Click "Stop Recording" when finished
  3. Download: Click "Download Video File" to save to your computer
  4. Upload: Go to your Admin Panel → Upload Video tab
  5. Select: Choose the downloaded video file
  6. Submit: Fill in title, description, thumbnail and click "Upload Video"
💡 Tips for best results:
• Use "Low" quality for smaller file size (recommended)
• Keep recordings under 2 minutes for quick upload
• Close unnecessary tabs/windows before recording
• The video will save as a .webm file (works in all browsers)
