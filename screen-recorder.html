📌 How to add screen recording to your course:

  1. Click "Start Recording" and choose which screen/window to share
  2. Record your content (keep under 1 minute for smaller file size)
  3. Click "Stop Recording" when finished
  4. Click "Download Video File" to save the recording to your computer
  5. Go back to your Admin Panel → Upload Video tab
  6. Select the downloaded video file and upload normally
⚠️ Note: Due to browser security restrictions, the video cannot be automatically uploaded to your HTTP server. Simply download the file and then upload it manually using the "Upload Video" tab in your admin panel.
💡 Tips for smaller file size:
- Use "Low" quality setting
- Keep recordings under 1 minute
- Close unnecessary windows/tabs before recording
- Files under 3MB will upload fastest
